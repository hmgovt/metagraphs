THE TAO CORE

SUBNET 0 · ROOT · WHERE EMISSION STARTS

Every ~12 seconds the chain mints new TAO — about 3,600 a day since the December 2025 halving cut the block reward to 0.5 TAO. That inflation is the city's entire payroll.

Since dTAO, no committee decides who gets what. Each subnet's share of emission follows the market for its alpha token: stake flows in, share rises. Here, one Yuma epoch (~72 min) is compressed to 36 seconds — the amber arc is the clock, and the ring that sweeps the city is the payout.

Emission injects TAO into each subnet's pool; participants are paid in that subnet's alpha, split 41% miners · 41% validators & their stakers · 18% owner.

The question the city asks: does emission reach towers the market independently values — or towers that exist only because they're paid to? That's the conviction lens, and the backed-emission number bottom-right.
